HOW TO PLAY

WASD / arrows — walk or drive. Shift — run on foot, NOS boost in a car.

E — steal / enter a car, get out, open the garage or a shop.

Mouse click — shoot (on foot). Q (hold) or 1–5 — weapon wheel.

M — start the next story mission. R — change radio station.

F — save. Esc — pause. Missions pay money and respect; respect unlocks a shop discount, a free Vesper GT, stronger crew and gold paint.

Garage: buy cars and upgrades with mission money. Races: follow the yellow rings and beat the par time. Crew: Mara (races) and Dario (muscle) join you as the story goes on.
