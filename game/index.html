VERDE BAY

— Sunset Heist —

One driver. Six jobs. A whole city that wants him caught.

Cole Vega came back to Verde Bay broke. Mara Voss, the fixer who runs the garage on 5th, has work — if Cole can drive, steal, and disappear faster than the VBPD can catch him.

An original game. Verde Bay, its people and its story are invented — no real city, no borrowed game.

How to play

WASD — walk, or drive when you're in a car.
E — get in or out of a car; start the next job at Mara's Garage.
Mouse — aim. Click — shoot.
Space — brake. Esc — pause.

Steal cars, lose the cops, run the six jobs for Mara. Grab a taxi fare for extra cash on the side. Your progress saves after every job.
