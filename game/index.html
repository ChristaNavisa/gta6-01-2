VERDE BAY

— Sunset Heist —

One driver. Six jobs. A whole city that wants him caught.

Cole Vega came back to Verde Bay broke. Mara Voss, the fixer who runs the garage on 5th, has work — if Cole can drive, steal, and disappear faster than the VBPD can catch him.

On foot: WASD to walk, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, click to shoot.
In a car: E to get in or out (any car is yours — if the law is watching, that's a crime), W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake.
Wanted: stars rise when you steal, hit, or shoot. Lose the cops to cool off. Get caught on foot and you're BUSTED.
Jobs: follow the yellow marker. Finish all six jobs to own the city. Taxi cabs pay fares on the side. Progress saves automatically.

An original game. Verde Bay, its people and its story are invented — no real city, no borrowed game.
